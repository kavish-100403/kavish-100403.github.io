01 About

Hi, I'm Kavish Rajpopat, a master's student in Computer Science at NYU Tandon graduating in May 2027. I'm looking for full time roles in software engineering, machine learning, and applied AI.

I co-authored IEEE published research on explainable fake news detection, where I built a CNN, LSTM, and BiLSTM ensemble that reached 95.2% accuracy on more than 600,000 articles and used SHAP to make its decisions transparent. On the software side, I've had two pull requests merged into Darnit, an open source Python CLI from the NYU Secure Systems Lab, and I built Snickr, a Flask and PostgreSQL collaboration app with automated tests, CI, and Docker.

I enjoy taking a project from raw data all the way to a tested, usable product.
